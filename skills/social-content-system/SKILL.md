# Social content system

A marketing workflow by Vibiz. Produce content the business can maintain and evaluate against its actual goal.

## Establish a repeatable source of useful material

Read the audience, offer, voice, goal and existing content. Identify what the team can genuinely show: product use, customer questions, founder expertise, demonstrations, common objections or permissioned customer stories. Ask about available footage, design capacity and review time if those constrain the plan.

Choose platforms according to audience behavior and the formats the team can produce. Set cadence from capacity. Do not equate posting everywhere with a strategy, invent an ideal posting time or rely on unsupported algorithm claims. Check current platform documentation when specifications matter.

Organize topics around buyer questions and desired actions. Give each topic a job: discovery, understanding, trust, objection resolution or conversion. Repetition of the offer can be useful; repeated empty slogans are not a content system.

## Deliver drafts and production instructions

For the requested planning period, provide a calendar with platform, audience question, format, hook, main idea, evidence/source, CTA, required asset, owner and draft status. Include enough complete posts to demonstrate the voice and execution quality; if the user requests a full calendar of drafts, write them all.

Adapt one source idea to each platform's consumption pattern. A video opening, carousel sequence and text post need different constructions. Preserve the core claim while changing the execution. Distinguish post copy from creator notes and clearly mark any example numbers or missing evidence.

Use real product details and truthful demonstrations. Do not fabricate results, conversations or customer endorsement. Obtain permission before using nonpublic customer material. Supply a practical reuse path from an existing asset rather than adding unnecessary production work.

## Close the feedback loop

Choose metrics based on the stated goal: relevant reach for discovery, qualified visits or inquiries for intent, and tracked conversions for acquisition. Separate platform-reported and first-party results when both exist. Describe tracking gaps instead of claiming attribution without evidence.

Set a review interval and rules for retaining, revising or retiring a topic. Compare like formats and account for distribution/sample differences. Deliver the next production batch and an actionable learning question, not a promise of virality.
